The Framework

The hub of campus
entrepreneurship

The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Cell (IEDC) at GPTC Attingal acts as the primary node for the Kerala Startup Mission. We bridge the gap between technical education and market-ready innovation.

Student-run innovation hub
IEDC is entirely led by students. Faculty advisors guide the cell, but execution is driven by the student executive team.
Backed by Kerala Government
Supported by Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM), giving members access to state-level resources, grants, and the startup network.
Open to every department
Whether you are in Hardware, Mechanical, Electrical, or Automobile — our programmes are designed for all.
Bridge to Industry
We bring real problems, mentors, and industry opportunities into the campus through workshops and collaborations.
